Kiri privacy policy

Applies to the Kiri Android app and the web version at rynnakkosika.github.io/kiri. Last updated 5 November 2026.

Kiri is a racing game you play on your own device. It has no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't collect or sell personal information.

What stays on your device

The game saves your current race, your difficulty choice and your sound setting in your device's local storage so you can continue later. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing the browser's site data deletes it.

Playing with a friend (optional)

The online mode is used only if you open Play with a friend. Then:

  • The game downloads the PeerJS library from unpkg.com.
  • The free public PeerJS service (peerjs.com) introduces your device to your friend's device. To do this it handles a random game code and your device's network (IP) address.
  • After that, race data (moves, stable and horse names you type) goes directly between the two devices over an encrypted WebRTC connection. It isn't stored on any server by Kiri.

These services have their own privacy practices. Kiri doesn't send them anything except what the connection needs.

Permissions

The Android app only uses the internet permission, which the online mode needs.

Children

Kiri doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page. For questions, open an issue at github.com/rynnakkosika/kiri/issues.
